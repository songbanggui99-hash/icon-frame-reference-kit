# Pack installation checklist

- Keep PNG platform files separate from ICO entries.
- Match every manifest src and HTML href to its deployed URL.
- Give maskable artwork its own opaque background and safe zone.
- An RGBA encoding label alone does not establish transparent pixels.
- Byte-preserved PNG extraction differs from rendering a legacy DIB entry.
